Privacy Policy — Pach Waqt (পাঁচ ওয়াক্ত)

Developer: Code Inherit
App: Pach Waqt — package com.codeinherit.pachwaqt (Android), bundle com.codeinherit.pachwaqt (iOS)
Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.codeinherit.pachwaqt
Effective date: 4 November 2026
Contact: s.anwar369@gmail.com

Pach Waqt has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no server of its own. Everything you see in the app — prayer times, calendar, tracker, tasbih, zakat, duas — is calculated and stored on your phone. The only data that ever leaves your device is anonymous crash and usage diagnostics, described below.

1. Data that stays on your device

The app keeps the following only in your phone's private app storage. It is never uploaded by the app and is deleted when you uninstall it:

  • your chosen district and, if you used the GPS button, the coordinates found at that moment;
  • settings (language, numerals, theme, notification choices, prayer-time and azan adjustments, Hijri offset, sehri margin);
  • tasbih counts and namaz tracker entries you record;
  • zakat calculator inputs (only while the screen is open; nothing is saved).

If you export tracker data as a CSV file or share a monthly timetable image, the file goes only to the app you choose in the share sheet.

2. Location

Location is read only on the device and only when you tap "GPS দিয়ে নির্ণয়" (Detect with GPS). The app reads your position once, finds the nearest district, stores the coordinates locally for prayer-time calculation, and stops. You can replace this at any time by picking a district manually. The app never reads location in the background and never transmits it.

3. Diagnostics we receive (Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase)

To find and fix bugs, the app includes Google's Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase SDKs. They send the following to Google servers on our behalf:

  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): stack trace, app version, device model, operating system version, device state at the time of the crash (free memory, storage, orientation, battery), a random Crashlytics installation identifier, and the Firebase installation ID. Sent only when the app crashes or hits an internal error.
  • Usage statistics (Analytics): automatically logged events such as app open, screen view, session length, app update, device model, OS version, app version, language, coarse country/region derived from the IP address at the time of the request and a random Firebase app-instance ID. The app does not use the Android advertising ID: the permission is removed from the app and advertising-ID collection is switched off in Analytics. We do not log any custom events, user IDs, names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, prayer data, tracker data or location through Analytics.

What we do with it: count crashes and active installs, see which screens are used, and prioritise fixes. We do not use this data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not combine it with any personal information (we have none).

Retention: Google keeps Crashlytics crash data for 90 days. Analytics identifier-level data is kept for 2 months (Google's default); aggregated reports may be kept longer. Google processes this data as our service provider under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase data processing terms. Data is encrypted in transit.

How to stop it: diagnostics are sent only while the app is installed. Uninstalling the app stops all collection; reinstalling generates new random identifiers, so earlier data cannot be linked to you.

4. Notifications

Azan and reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your phone. No push-notification service is used, and no notification content is sent anywhere.

6. Third-party links

The app links to our other apps on Google Play, to this policy, and to open-source licence texts. Opening a link takes you to Google Play or your browser, which have their own privacy policies.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address and the effective date is updated. Material changes (for example, collecting any new kind of data) will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

Code Inherit — s.anwar369@gmail.com. We reply to privacy questions and data requests within 30 days.
